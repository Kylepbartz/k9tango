Tango

Police K9

★

Tango is a retired police K9 who faithfully served and protected the community of Menomonee Falls for six years. Now he’s facing a new battle — cancer.

Tango’s handler and family have asked that all donations go to Project K-9 Hero, the nonprofit supporting his medical care. Gifts sent to @K9Tango on Venmo are forwarded to them.

Project K-9 Hero is a national 501(c)(3) nonprofit that helps retired police K9s with medical care. It relies on donations to operate.

Care for K9 heroes like Tango is made possible by the generosity of caring people. Every gift helps make sure these loyal partners get the care they deserve after a lifetime of service.
